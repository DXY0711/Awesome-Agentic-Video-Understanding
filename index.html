03 · State-space paradigms

How does an agent represent a video while it reasons?

The four paradigms form a progression from selected evidence units to continuously revised world models.

I

Bag of frames

Selected frames, clips, shots, or candidate segments.

Efficient · query-focused
II

Temporal sequence

Ordered observations and relations along an unfolding timeline.

Procedural · temporal
III

Graph of entities

Persistent entities linked across time, space, and modalities.

Relational · retrievable
IV

Evolving world state

A partial, time-indexed state revised as new evidence arrives.

Causal · proactive
